# Munch



HOW TO PLAY



\- Use the WASD keys to point your food eater in one of four directions.



\- You cannot move him; he will always remain at the screen center.



\- Face him in the direction of the incoming foods so he can eat them (they need to go in his mouth) and score a point for every food he eats.



\- Be wary, though; for every food you miss, you lose one point instead.

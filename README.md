# Munch



HOW TO PLAY

- Use the WASD keys to point Munch (your character) in one of four directions.
- 
- You cannot move Munch; he will always remain at the screen center.
- 
- Face Munch in the direction of the incoming foods so he can eat them (they need to go in his mouth) and score a point for every food he eats.
- 
- Be wary, though; for every food you miss, you lose one point instead.
- 
- Also, when a piece of food is being eaten by Munch, he cannot turn (he can turn while his mouth is opening, though).
